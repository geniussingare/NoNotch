What's new in noNotch

0.2.0

October 31, 2026 Latest

Your day, in the notch: tasks, a focus timer, a daily notepad, insights, Apple Reminders sync, and a built-in MCP server for Claude and Cursor.

Tasks

  • New A Tasks tab with natural-language entry: “call mom tomorrow at 5pm every week #family !high” sets the day, reminder, repeat, project and priority, previewed as chips while you type.
  • New Task details: priority, project, day, reminder time, repeat, steps and notes.
  • New Repeating tasks: daily, weekdays, weekly on a day, or monthly. Finishing one schedules the next.
  • New Upcoming: a week strip to plan future days, plus Later.
  • New When a reminder is due, the notch opens with a chime and Done / Snooze 5 min.
  • New Unfinished tasks roll forward to today at midnight.
  • New To do / Done switch, double-click to rename, drag to reorder.

Focus, notepad and insights

  • New Focus timer as a countdown or stopwatch, with or without a task. Add five minutes, pause, and it waits while your Mac sleeps.
  • New The running timer stays beside the notch, with dotted PAUSED and DONE states.
  • New Ring, digital or minimal clock styles, session length and an optional short break, in Settings → Focus.
  • New A daily notepad page. ⌘↩ turns the current line into a task.
  • New Insights: tasks done and focus time for the last seven days, peak hours and a weekly summary.

Sync, voice and AI

  • New Optional two-way sync with a “noNotch” list in Apple Reminders, reaching iPhone and Apple Watch. Off until you turn it on.
  • New Dictation in the task composer and the notepad, recognised on your Mac.
  • New On macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, new tasks get a suggested priority and project from the on-device model. Tap to apply, or dismiss.
  • New Built-in MCP server. Settings → AI agents connects Claude Code, the Claude app or Cursor in one click, with a Restart button and “Try asking” prompts.

Home and polish

  • New Customizable Home: show, hide, resize and reorder the Clock, Agenda, Focus and Today's Tasks cards.
  • Improved “Up next” is now Agenda, showing your next event and next task.
  • New A new menu bar icon: a tiny island in the app's colours that shows focus progress, minutes left and a dot for reminders and notifications.
  • New A WELCOME BACK greeting beside the notch on launch.
  • New Check for Updates… in the menu bar, and a glass Update button in the notch when a new version is out.
  • New Report a Bug in the menu bar and Settings.
  • Improved The notch moves out of the way while Settings is open.
  • Improved Optional Reminders and Microphone permissions on the welcome screen, which you can skip.
  • Fixed The notch fits flush at scaled resolutions such as More Space, with no hairline or sliver.
  • Fixed With a 12-hour clock, the Home time no longer overlaps the Agenda.

0.1.0

October 30, 2026

First release: a Dynamic Island for the Mac notch.

  • New Now Playing for music and video, with live lyrics, AirPlay and ±15 s skips.
  • New Notifications, live activities and a volume and brightness HUD in the notch.
  • New Quick controls, shelf, clipboard, notes, calendar, weather and a camera mirror.
  • New Liquid Glass design, and a pull-cord for displays without a notch.
